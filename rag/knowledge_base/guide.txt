
Beginner Fitness Workout Guide

1. Walking
Walking is a beginner-friendly physical activity.
A beginner can start with 15 to 20 minutes of walking.
The duration can gradually increase according to fitness level.

2. Squats
Squats help strengthen the lower body muscles.
Beginners can start with 2 sets of 10 repetitions.
Maintain proper posture while performing squats.

3. Push-ups
Push-ups help strengthen the chest, shoulders and arms.
Beginners can start with wall push-ups or knee push-ups.
Perform the exercise with controlled movements.

4. Plank
Planks help strengthen the core muscles.
Beginners can start by holding the position for 10 to 15 seconds.
Maintain a straight body position.

5. Rest and Recovery
Rest is important for muscle recovery.
Beginners should avoid training the same muscle groups intensely every day.
Adequate sleep and recovery support fitness progress.
